Aches and pains massage can help with

Not sure which massage to book in Nakuru? Start with what bothers you most.

Lower back pain

Long hours sitting or standing can leave the lower back tight and sore. Deep tissue and Swedish massage can help ease muscle tension around the lower back.

Neck and shoulder tension

Desk work, phone use and stress tighten the neck and shoulders, and can bring tension headaches with them. A massage can help loosen stiff muscles and leave you more comfortable.

Sore muscles from sport or the gym

Sports massage works on tight, tired muscles after training or a match, and can help you feel looser and ready to move again.

Stiff, tight muscles

The warmth of smooth heated stones helps tight, stiff muscles relax so the therapist can work more comfortably.

Pregnancy aches

A prenatal massage is gentle and set up for pregnancy. It can ease a sore back and tired legs. Tell your therapist how far along you are, and check with your doctor first.

Stress, tiredness and poor sleep

Slow, calming strokes with aromatherapy oils can help your body switch off, and many people feel calmer and sleep better afterwards.

Massage is for relaxation and comfort and does not replace medical care. If you have severe or sudden pain, numbness, a fever, a recent injury or a medical condition, see a doctor first and tell your therapist before your session.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about massage, prices and booking at The Massage Palour in Nakuru.

Where is The Massage Palour in Nakuru?
We are at Naivas Supercentre, along the Nakuru–Nairobi Highway in Nakuru. Tap Get directions in the Visit us section to open the map.
What time do you close?
We close at 9 pm. Message us on WhatsApp on 0711 867466 to confirm the time that suits you before you come.
How do I book a massage?
Tap Book now, choose your treatment, pick a date and time, and send the request on WhatsApp. You can also call or message 0711 867466.
How much does a massage cost in Nakuru?
Deep tissue massage is Ksh 3,500 for 1 hour and Ksh 6,000 for 2 hours. Swedish massage is Ksh 3,000 for 1 hour and Ksh 5,500 for 2 hours. Aromatherapy and sports massage are Ksh 4,000 for 1 hour, prenatal massage is Ksh 4,000 for 1 hour 30 minutes, hot stone massage is Ksh 5,000 for 1 hour 45 minutes, and the 4 hands tandem massage is Ksh 6,000 for 1 hour 45 minutes.
What is the difference between Swedish and deep tissue massage?
Swedish massage uses long, gentle strokes and is mainly for relaxation. Deep tissue massage uses firmer pressure to work on tight muscles, so it suits knots and lasting tension. Tell your therapist how much pressure you like.
Do you offer couples massage?
Yes. The price depends on the package you choose, so message us on WhatsApp to pick a package and a time for two.
Is prenatal massage available?
Yes. A prenatal massage takes 1 hour 30 minutes and costs Ksh 4,000. Please tell your therapist how many weeks pregnant you are, and check with your doctor first if you have any concerns.
What is a 4 hands tandem massage?
Two therapists work on you at the same time, so you get the strokes of a full massage from both sides. It takes 1 hour 45 minutes and costs Ksh 6,000.
Which body scrubs do you offer?
We offer normal, coffee, argan oil, lemon, salt and gold scrubs. Coffee scrub is Ksh 2,500 and the others are Ksh 2,000 each.
How much are waxing and shaving?
Underarms waxing is Ksh 700, bikini Ksh 1,000, Brazilian Ksh 1,500, full arm Ksh 1,500, half leg Ksh 2,000, full leg Ksh 3,000 and full body Ksh 7,000. Shaving is Ksh 500 for underarms, Ksh 700 for bikini and Ksh 5,000 for full body.
Do you sell gift vouchers?
Yes. Gift vouchers come in Ksh 3,500, Ksh 5,000 and Ksh 8,000, and suit birthdays, Mother’s Day, Father’s Day, Men’s Day, Women’s Day and anniversaries.
How should I prepare for a massage?
Arrive a few minutes early and avoid a heavy meal just before. Tell your therapist where it hurts, and about any injury, allergy to oils or pregnancy so they can adjust the treatment.
